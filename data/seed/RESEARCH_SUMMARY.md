- **Black Hawk** — STRs completely prohibited (Ordinance 2024-4, February 14, 2024)
- **Routt County (Unincorporated)** — STRs banned in all residential zones (commercial zones and B&B/guest ranch permits may apply)
- **Lakewood** — Whole-home STRs under 30 days prohibited; only owner-occupied hosted stays allowed

## Notes for Human Verifiers

1. **All fees may have changed** — verify current fees directly with each jurisdiction
2. **Permit numbers must appear in ads** in many jurisdictions — confirm which ones
3. **Tax obligations are separate** from permit requirements — all Colorado STRs must collect state sales tax, and applicable local lodging taxes
4. **HOA rules** may prohibit STRs even where local government allows them
5. The CO state law (HB 23-1117 and related) governs the state-level baseline; local jurisdictions cannot preemptively ban STRs statewide but can limit them
6. **Routt County** STR ban status should be verified — regulations may have been updated in 2024–2025
7. **Buena Vista** moratorium end date needs to be confirmed with the town
8. **Lakewood** 2024 ordinance review outcome should be verified
